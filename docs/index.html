Newsical

The day's world and U.S. news, sung. A satirical musical recap for teens.

RSS feed

Newsical is a teen-friendly, lightly satirical musical recap of the day's most important world and U.S. news, released weekday mornings in time for the drive to school. Each episode turns a handful of real stories into short original songs and character scenes: what happened, why it matters, and what is still uncertain. Stories are researched and fact-checked against a published source ledger with links, and every episode page includes that ledger and a transcript. Coverage aims to be accurate and nonpartisan; the jokes aim at bureaucracy and absurdity, never at people in danger. AI disclosure: the music, singing and spoken voices in this show are generated by AI models, and the scripts are written with AI assistance; no real person's voice is imitated.
